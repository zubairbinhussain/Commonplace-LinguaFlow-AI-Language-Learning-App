# Commonplace (LinguaFlow) — AI Language Learning App

> Speech recognition & NLP-powered conversational practice, replacing passive study with real-time AI conversation.

🔗 [lingua-flow-ai-lab.vercel.app](https://lingua-flow-ai-lab.vercel.app)

## Overview

LinguaFlow is an AI-powered language learning platform built to replace traditional passive study methods with real-time, AI-driven conversational practice. The platform uses speech recognition, generative AI, and adaptive learning technology to deliver personalized language learning experiences that adjust dynamically to each learner's performance, pace, and proficiency level.

## Problem

Traditional language learning apps relied on repetitive exercises, static content, and passive study that produced slow fluency progress. Learners had no way to practice real conversation in a low-pressure environment outside of expensive tutors or language classes. Pronunciation feedback was absent or generic, and learning paths were fixed regardless of individual progress — causing advanced learners to disengage and beginners to fall behind.

## Solution

Built a conversational AI language learning platform powered by generative AI and real-time speech recognition. The AI engages learners in natural conversations that match their current level, provides instant feedback on pronunciation accuracy, and adapts the difficulty and content of each session based on performance data. Personalized learning paths are generated and updated continuously, ensuring every learner follows a route optimized for their specific goals and progress — scaling to unlimited concurrent learners without additional tutors or instructors.

## Responsibilities

- Designed the conversational AI interaction engine for real-time language practice sessions
- Built the speech recognition system for pronunciation analysis and instant accuracy feedback
- Developed the adaptive learning path algorithm based on performance tracking and proficiency assessment
- Integrated generative AI for dynamic, context-aware conversation generation across difficulty levels
- Deployed the platform on scalable cloud infrastructure for concurrent multi-user access
